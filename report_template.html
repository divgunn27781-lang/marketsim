Risk metrics

Backtest: rolling 750-day GARCH 99% VaR. Kupiec p below 0.05 means the exception count is rejected as too high or low.

Stress scenarios

A global shock of 3, 5 and 8 standard deviations, passed to each market through its beta on global down-days, plus its worst historical window.

Limitations

  • Same-day closes are used for every market, so time-zone differences are not corrected.
  • GARCH uses Gaussian quasi-maximum likelihood. Fat tails are handled by filtered historical simulation.
  • Hedge fund, private equity and investment banking figures are stored in the database for context. They are quarterly or annual, so they do not yet feed the return model.
  • This is an analytical prototype, not investment advice or a validated production risk system.
